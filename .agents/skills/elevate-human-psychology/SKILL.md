# Elevate Human Psychology Skill (V4.1)

## Purpose
Establishes an ethical human psychology framework for interior design content and visual commerce. Applies proven cognitive principles to make luxury spatial choices intuitive, engaging, and clear—while strictly enforcing transparency, authenticity, and zero deceptive dark patterns.

---

## 1. Ethical Psychology Framework (SECTION 20)

Apply ethical cognitive principles to enhance reader confidence:
- **Clarity & Visual Hierarchy:** Guide attention through spatial breathing room, scale, and contrast.
- **Cognitive Ease:** Structure information into scannable chunks to minimize mental friction.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Present high-level spatial insights first; expose granular details on scroll/interaction.
- **Curiosity & Relevance:** Open compelling spatial dilemmas early (e.g. rug scale mistakes) and resolve them contextually.
- **Comparison Clarity & Confidence:** Provide objective comparison matrices to build decision confidence.
- **Friction Reduction:** Eliminate clutter and obscure layout patterns that impede reading flow.

> [!CAUTION]
> **STRICT PROHIBITION ON DECEPTIVE PATTERNS:**
> - DO NOT use deceptive scarcity ("Only 2 left in stock").
> - DO NOT use fake urgency ("Sale ends in 10 minutes").
> - DO NOT use fake social proof ("Over 500 readers bought this today").
> - DO NOT use hidden commercial intent or misleading CTAs.
> - DO NOT manipulate or mislead the reader under any circumstances.

---

## 2. Ethical Conversion Sequence (SECTION 21)

The visual narrative of every page MUST support the 6-stage conversion journey:

$$\text{DISCOVER} \rightarrow \text{UNDERSTAND} \rightarrow \text{DESIRE} \rightarrow \text{EXPLORE} \rightarrow \text{COMPARE} \rightarrow \text{SHOP}$$

### For Affiliate & Commerce Content:
$$\text{Editorial Context} \rightarrow \text{Product Discovery} \rightarrow \text{Useful Comparison} \rightarrow \text{Product Detail} \rightarrow \text{Shop The Look} \rightarrow \text{Amazon CTA}$$

- **Editorial Value Primary:** Do NOT turn every section into a sales pitch. Editorial intelligence and spatial advice must always lead. Commercial CTAs enter naturally once desire and understanding are established.

---

## 3. Core Psychological Principles

### 1. Attention & Visual Focus
- **Information Scent:** Provide clear visual breadcrumbs so readers know they are on the right path.
- **Pattern Interrupt:** Use visual cards, diagrams, or high-contrast callouts to break up long copy blocks.

### 2. Cognitive Load & Decision Architecture
- **Choice Architecture:** Limit options per category (3–5 max) to prevent choice paralysis.
- **Endowment Effect (Mental Ownership):** Help readers visualize products inside their home using room diagrams before recommending purchase.
