# Elevate Pinterest Growth Skill

## Overview

The `elevate-pinterest-growth` skill defines the distribution architecture, board taxonomy, publishing cadence, and long-term content amplification strategy for ElevateLivingCo on Pinterest.

## Core Growth Principles

1. **Board Taxonomy & Semantic Mapping:**
   - Boards act as thematic collections that establish context for Pinterest's visual index.
   - Pins must be pinned to the most specific, relevant board first.
   - Board titles and descriptions must be clear, keyword-aligned, and free of generic fluff.
2. **Fresh Pin Strategy:**
   - Pinterest prioritizes "Fresh Pins" (new images or new overlays) over re-pinning identical existing images.
   - For every published article, launch a batch of 3–5 visually distinct Fresh Pins.
3. **Publishing Cadence & Spacing:**
   - Avoid burst publishing. Space Pins targeting the same destination URL by at least 72 hours.
   - Maintain a consistent daily/weekly posting velocity to signal account activity to Pinterest distribution algorithms.
4. **Anti-Spam & Quality Controls:**
   - No rapid duplicate pinning.
   - No cross-pinning to irrelevant boards.
   - No deceptive redirect URLs.
