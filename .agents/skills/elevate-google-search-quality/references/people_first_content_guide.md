# People-First Content Writing Guide for ElevateLivingCo
## Operational Implementation of Google Search Central's Content Quality Guidelines

---

### 1. The Core Mindset: Solving Dilemmas for Humans First

Google's automated ranking systems reward content that genuinely satisfies the searcher's intent. When creating or revising articles for ElevateLivingCo, follow this three-stage mental test:

1. **Before writing:** What specific frustration or constraint does the reader face?
   - *Example:* "I live in a 500 sq ft apartment, and my living room feels cramped because my furniture blocks natural light."
2. **During writing:** Am I providing concrete, actionable spatial advice, or generic fluff?
   - *Fluff:* "Make sure your space is bright and airy by choosing nice furniture."
   - *People-First Value:* "Opt for seating with 5 to 7 inches of ground clearance. Elevated legs allow eyesight and natural daylight to flow uninterrupted across the floor plane, signaling to the brain that the room is larger than its perimeter."
3. **After writing:** Does the reader leave with a clear plan, or do they need to Google the same question again?

---

### 2. Operationalizing "Who, How, and Why"

#### WHO Created This Content?
* **Visible Authorship:**
  ```html
  <div class="article-meta">
    <span class="author">By Vikram Chennamsetty</span> · 
    <span class="role">ElevateLivingCo Editorial Team</span> · 
    <span class="date">Updated October 2026</span>
  </div>
  ```
* **Clear About Page Linking:** The author name should link directly to `about.html` or an author profile detailing design background, editorial philosophy, and contact channels.
* **No Synthetic Authority:** Never use fake personas (e.g. "By Sarah Jenkins, Master Architect of 20 Years") if Sarah Jenkins is not a real, verifiable contributor.

#### HOW Was This Content Created?
* **Explain the Methodology:**
  *"To compile this guide, our team audited 14 compact living room layouts, tested sightline clearance with 3 different sofa leg profiles, and curated products that meet strict scale ratios for urban apartments."*
* **Transparency on Sourcing:**
  Explain how materials, measurements, and recommendations were verified. If AI-assisted tools were used for outline generation or grammar, explicit human curation, measurement validation, and styling judgment must govern the final copy.

#### WHY Was This Content Created?
* **The Test:** If you strip away all affiliate links and Google rankings, is this article still immensely helpful to a person moving into a small apartment?
* If the answer is yes, the "Why" is aligned with Google Search Central. If the answer is no, restructure the content.

### 3. The 16 Self-Assessment Questions in Practice

| Google Self-Assessment Question | ElevateLivingCo Application Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Original information or reporting?** | Include unique room layout formulas (e.g., 2/3 sofa-to-wall anchor ratio), original diagrams, or custom spatial breakdowns. |
| **Comprehensive description?** | Cover layout, lighting, vertical storage, textile scale, and rug boundaries in a single coherent guide. |
| **Insight beyond the obvious?** | Explain *why* certain designs fail (e.g., why buying miniature furniture can actually make a room look more cluttered). |
| **Not just rewriting competitors?** | Introduce original interior styling terminology, architectural perspectives, and tangible room setups. |
| **Descriptive, non-clickbait headline?** | Avoid "You Won't Believe These 12 Hacks". Use "Small Apartment Living Room Decor Ideas: 12 Ways to Make Compact Spaces Look Bigger". |
| **Bookmark/Share worthy?** | Format actionable takeaways in clear callouts, bulleted checklists, and styling formulas. |
| **Editorial publication standard?** | Consistent typography (Cormorant Garamond / Inter), refined dark luxury aesthetic, flawless grammar, and zero broken links. |
| **Factual accuracy?** | Verified furniture dimensions, correct Amazon ASINs, accurate ground clearances, and truthful pricing tiers. |
| **No manipulative urgency?** | Zero fake countdowns, zero "buy now before prices spike" claims. |
| **Audience alignment?** | Specifically addresses renters, small apartments, high-ceiling lofts, or urban studios. |
