# ElevateLivingCo Pinterest KPI Definitions & Standards (V1.0)

**Source:** Official Pinterest Business Analytics Help Center Definitions & Platform Guidelines  
**Verification Date:** September 28, 2026  
**Scope:** Primary Metric Definitions, Calculations, and Interpretations for ElevateLivingCo

---

## 1. Official Platform Metric Definitions

### 1. IMPRESSIONS
- **Platform Definition:** The total number of times your Pin was shown on screen in the home feed, search results, category feeds, or related pin recommendations.
- **Source:** Pinterest Analytics Help Center.
- **Interpretation:** Base reach metric. Used as the denominator for all rate-based efficiency KPIs.

### 2. PIN CLICKS (Formerly Close-ups)
- **Platform Definition:** The total number of clicks or taps on your Pin that open the expanded Pin detailed view or closeup.
- **Source:** Pinterest Analytics Help Center.
- **Interpretation:** Measures visual curiosity and high-intent engagement before an outbound visit.

### 3. OUTBOUND CLICKS (Formerly Link Clicks)
- **Platform Definition:** The total number of times a user clicks on the destination URL link within your Pin, redirecting them to an external site (e.g. `elevatelivingco.me`).
- **Source:** Pinterest Analytics Help Center.
- **Interpretation:** Primary conversion driver for article traffic and downstream affiliate monetization.

### 4. SAVES (Formerly Repins)
- **Platform Definition:** The total number of times users saved your Pin asset to one of their personal Pinterest boards.
- **Source:** Pinterest Analytics Help Center.
- **Interpretation:** Measures high-intent bookmarking, long-tail viral distribution, and aspirational resonance.

### 5. ENGAGEMENTS
- **Platform Definition:** The total combined count of all interactions with your Pin. Includes Pin clicks, Outbound clicks, Saves, and carousel card swipes.
- **Source:** Pinterest Analytics Help Center.
- **Interpretation:** Measure of total audience activity on the Pin creative.

---

## 2. Calculated Efficiency Rate KPIs

### 6. ENGAGEMENT RATE
- **Formula:** 
  $$\text{ENGAGEMENT\_RATE} = \left(\frac{\text{TOTAL ENGAGEMENTS}}{\text{IMPRESSIONS}}\right) \times 100$$
- **Platform Definition:** The percentage of total impressions that resulted in an interaction with your Pin.
- **Interpretation:** Indicates overall creative resonance and feed stopping power.

### 7. SAVE RATE
- **Formula:** 
  $$\text{SAVE\_RATE} = \left(\frac{\text{SAVES}}{\text{IMPRESSIONS}}\right) \times 100$$
- **Interpretation:** Evaluates how "saveable" the Pin is. High save rates signify timeless reference value and high visual utility.

### 8. OUTBOUND CLICK RATE (CTR)
- **Formula:** 
  $$\text{OUTBOUND\_CLICK\_RATE} = \left(\frac{\text{OUTBOUND CLICKS}}{\text{IMPRESSIONS}}\right) \times 100$$
- **Interpretation:** Evaluates how effectively the Pin turns attention into external blog traffic. Driven primarily by headline clarity, curiosity gap strength, and call-to-action relevance.

---

## 3. Data Integrity & Reporting Rules

1. **Platform Alignment:** Never alter or override official Pinterest Analytics definitions with third-party assumptions.
2. **Date Range Consistency:** Always compare metrics collected over identical timeframe windows (e.g. 7-day, 14-day, 30-day post-publish windows).
3. **Organic vs. Paid Isolation:** Separate organic Pin performance completely from promoted/paid ad campaigns.
4. **Missing Values Standard:** When data has not yet been fetched or indexation is pending, record as `N/A`.
