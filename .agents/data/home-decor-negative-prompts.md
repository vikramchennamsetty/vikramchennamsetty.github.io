# ElevateLivingCo — Home Decor Realism Negative Prompt Library

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-29  

Use these standardized negative prompt blocks to eliminate AI visual artifacts, unrealistic lighting, distorted architecture, and fake typography when generating home-decor imagery for Pinterest.

---

## 1. Master Negative Prompt Block (Copy & Use)

```text
CGI look, 3D digital render, octane render, video game graphics, plastic furniture, warped furniture legs, floating objects, impossible architecture, crooked vertical lines, tilted walls, bent shelves, malformed lamps, duplicated objects, duplicate book spines, impossible glass reflections, conflicting shadow directions, oversaturated colors, unnatural HDR bloom, artificial skin texture, fake text overlay, gibberish typography, watermark, signature, logo, deformed ceramic vases, melted metal fixtures, excessive blur, extreme depth of field bokeh, fisheye distortion, ultra wide angle warp, unrealistic room dimensions, impossible camera perspective, messy clutter, low-resolution textures, noisy artifacts.
```

## 2. Categorized Negative Exclusions

### A. Architectural & Perspective Realism
- `impossible architecture`, `crooked vertical lines`, `tilted walls`, `bent shelves`, `distorted doorway`, `warped floorboards`, `unrealistic ceiling height`, `fisheye lens distortion`, `ultra wide angle warp`, `impossible camera perspective`.

### B. Material & Surface Realism
- `plastic wood grain`, `synthetic leather`, `overly glossy surfaces`, `plastic furniture`, `melted metal fixtures`, `deformed ceramic vases`, `fake marble texture`, `flat untextured fabric`, `excessive HDR bloom`, `oversaturated colors`.

### C. Lighting & Shadow Realism
- `conflicting shadow directions`, `floating shadows`, `unshaded furniture contact`, `flat flash lighting`, `unnatural glow`, `impossible glass reflections`.

### D. Object & Furniture Integrity
- `floating objects`, `warped chair legs`, `malformed lamp shades`, `duplicated book spines`, `melted candle wax`, `deformed plant leaves`, `floating wall art`.

### E. AI Artifacts & Watermarks
- `CGI look`, `3D digital render`, `octane render`, `fake text overlay`, `gibberish typography`, `watermark`, `signature`, `logo`, `stock photo watermark`.
