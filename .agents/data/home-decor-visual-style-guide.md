# ElevateLivingCo — Home Decor Visual Style Guide & Niche Profiles

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-29  

This guide defines the architectural standards, visual style profiles, camera vocabulary, lighting physics, and product integration rules for generating editorial-grade Pinterest imagery.

---

## 1. Core Visual Style Profiles

### 🖤 DARK ACADEMIA
- **Color Palette:** Deep espresso walnut, oxblood leather, charcoal, forest green, warm antique gold.
- **Materials:** Aged solid walnut, full-grain vintage leather, antique brass, heavy clothbound paper, stoneware ceramics.
- **Furniture:** Vintage leather armchair, dark wood study desk, tall carved bookshelf, brass floor lamp.
- **Lighting:** Warm amber pool lighting (2700K), single shaded desk lamp, natural window side light, soft shadows.
- **Architectural Details:** Dark wainscoting, molded wood paneling, parquet wood floors, high ceilings.
- **Decor Objects:** Stacks of vintage clothbound books, brass globes, framed classical sketch prints, leather journals, ceramic mugs.
- **Photography Style:** 35mm eye-level interior editorial, shallow-to-medium depth of field, rich warm shadows.
- **Common Mistakes to Avoid:** Making the room look cold/gothic, using plastic or modern bright neon objects, painting walls flat jet black.

### 🏛️ NEO DECO / ART DECO
- **Color Palette:** Emerald green, deep navy, warm brushed brass, cream travertine, polished gold.
- **Materials:** Polished brass, white marble with grey veining, fluted glass, velvet upholstery, high-gloss lacquer.
- **Furniture:** Curved velvet sofa, geometric brass bar cart, round marble coffee table, fluted pedestal side table.
- **Lighting:** Warm diffused sconces, geometric glass pendant lighting, reflective metallic highlights.
- **Architectural Details:** Geometric wall molding, arched doorways, chevron hardwood floors.
- **Decor Objects:** Crystal glassware, sculptural brass candle holders, geometric ceramic vases, metallic trays.
- **Photography Style:** 24mm architectural straight-on shot, vertical lines aligned, high contrast, clean reflections.
- **Common Mistakes to Avoid:** Over-gilding, harsh chrome reflections, tacky 1920s party props instead of modern architectural Neo Deco.

### ✨ QUIET LUXURY / OLD MONEY
- **Color Palette:** Warm ivory, oat linen, taupe, soft sage, muted bronze, natural oak.
- **Materials:** Un-dyed Belgian linen, white oak, travertine stone, matte stoneware, wool throw blankets.
- **Furniture:** Low-profile linen sofa, solid oak coffee table, slipcovered lounge chair, minimal wood console.
- **Lighting:** Soft natural daylight, ambient parchment lamp shade glow, subtle shadow falloff.
- **Architectural Details:** Plaster walls, exposed white oak beams, neutral stone flooring.
- **Decor Objects:** Minimal ceramic donut vase, solitary art book, organic branches in stone crock.
- **Photography Style:** 28mm natural daylight editorial, soft contrast, airy breathing room.
- **Common Mistakes to Avoid:** Cluttered surfaces, high-contrast harsh shadows, cheap synthetic fabrics.

### 🛋️ SMALL APARTMENT / RENTER-FRIENDLY
- **Color Palette:** Crisp warm white, light natural oak, soft cream, muted terracotta, warm brass accents.
- **Materials:** Light oak, matte black steel, woven rattan, natural cotton, acrylic accents.
- **Furniture:** Leggy sofa elevated on thin legs, ultra-slim flip-drawer shoe cabinet, round glass coffee table, nesting tables.
- **Lighting:** Plug-in wall sconces, battery amber candles, slim floor lamps (zero ceiling wiring).
- **Architectural Details:** Standard apartment drywall, large bright window, light wood laminate flooring.
- **Decor Objects:** Floating picture ledges, woven storage baskets, vertical mirror, desk organizers.
- **Photography Style:** 24mm vertical interior perspective showing spatial flow and smart layout clearance.
- **Common Mistakes to Avoid:** Overcrowding floor space, heavy bulky furniture blockages, dark unlit corners.

### 🍂 COZY FALL / SEASONAL
- **Color Palette:** Warm amber, toasted cinnamon, creamy oat, burnt sienna, deep forest green.
- **Materials:** Chunky knit wool, boucle, aged wood, matte stoneware, dried eucalyptus, amber glass.
- **Furniture:** Plush armchair with throw blanket, wood side table, cozy reading nook.
- **Lighting:** Cordless warm amber fairy/table lamps, glowing candle tapers, golden hour window light.
- **Architectural Details:** Brick fireplace surround or cozy apartment window nook.
- **Decor Objects:** Ceramic mugs with steam, stacks of books, dried wheat/eucalyptus in stoneware, velvet pillows.
- **Photography Style:** 50mm close editorial vignette, warm golden hour lighting.
- **Common Mistakes to Avoid:** Orange plastic pumpkins, tacky Halloween props, oversaturated orange glow.

## 2. Realism Rules & Guidelines

1. **Furniture Proportions & Scale:** Furniture MUST maintain realistic human dimensions (e.g. coffee table height $\approx 16\text{--}18$ inches, seat height $\approx 18$ inches).
2. **Contact & Grounding:** All furniture legs and rugs MUST contact the floor cleanly with realistic contact shadows. Zero floating legs.
3. **Lighting Physics:** Light MUST have a single dominant directional source (e.g. daylight from window on left + warm accent glow from lamp on right).
4. **Material Realism:** Wood MUST show subtle grain; metals MUST show natural brushed/polished patina; glass MUST show subtle reflection and refraction.
5. **Text-Safe Zone Budget:** Reserve upper 30% or lower 30% of 2:3 vertical frame as clean, lower-contrast space for Pinterest text overlays.
