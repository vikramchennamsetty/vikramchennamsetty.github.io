# Elevate Pinterest Direct Affiliate Research Architecture & Intent Intelligence

## 1. Executive Overview & Provenance Standard

This document establishes the research methodology, search intent taxonomy, price band analysis, and evidence recording protocol for direct-to-Amazon affiliate Pinterest campaigns under `elevate-pinterest-direct-affiliate`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **RESEARCH EVIDENCE MANDATE:**
> Every research claim, keyword assertion, or market trend insight recorded in this system MUST include:
> 1. **Source:** Origin of evidence (e.g., Pinterest Search Suggestion, Amazon Catalog Audit, Decor Trend Synthesis).
> 2. **Date:** Timestamp of observation (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
> 3. **Claim:** Explicit empirical observation (e.g., "High volume visual search interest for irregular wall mirrors").
> 4. **Confidence / Evidence Level:** Categorized as `VERIFIED_EMPIRICAL`, `OBSERVED_TREND`, or `INFERRED_ANALOG`.
> 5. **Search Volume Rule:** Never invent search volumes. Set `SEARCH_VOLUME = NOT VERIFIED` if unmeasured.

## 2. Research Domain Requirements

### Domain 1: Pinterest Search Intent & Keyword Intelligence
- **Commercial Intent:** Queries where users are seeking specific home products (e.g., *"outdoor rug under 50"*, *"boho throw pillow covers"*).
- **Aesthetic Intent:** Queries focused on room styling mood (e.g., *"dark academia decor"*, *"organic modern living room"*).
- **Problem-Solving Intent:** Queries addressing room functional challenges (e.g., *"small entryway storage"*, *"renter friendly lighting"*).

### Domain 2: Seasonal Demand Curves & Lead-Time Windows
- **Spring Patio Refresh (Peak: March – May):** Focus on outdoor rugs, string lights, bistro seating. Lead time: January launch.
- **Fall & Autumn Warmth (Peak: August – October):** Focus on cozy textiles, warm lighting, autumn centerpieces, brass decor. Lead time: June launch.
- **Holiday Living & Gifting (Peak: October – December):** Focus on tableware, candle holders, luxury budget gifts. Lead time: August launch.
- **Evergreen Apartment Upgrades (Peak: Year-round):** Mirrors, coffee table decor, bookshelf accents.

### Domain 3: Amazon Product Opportunity & Commercial Sweet Spots
- **Price Band Analysis:**
  - **Impulse Range ($10 – $30):** Throw pillow covers, candle holders, decorative trays, small planters. (High volume conversion).
  - **Core Utility Sweet Spot ($30 – $75):** Outdoor rugs, string lights, wall mirrors, sconces. (Optimal affiliate revenue balance).
  - **High-Valence Accent ($75 – $150):** Accent tables, bar carts, small area rugs.
- **Review Profile Criteria:** Minimum 4.4/5.0 star rating with $\ge 500$ verified Amazon reviews.

### Domain 4: Renter-Friendly & Small-Space Solutions
- High priority given to non-damaging, plug-in, or multi-functional items:
  - No-drill lighting (plug-in sconces, string lights).
  - Washable/reversible lightweight rugs.
  - Wall mirrors using heavy-duty command strips or simple single-nail mounting.
  - Multi-room moveable accents (trays, book stands, accent lamps).
