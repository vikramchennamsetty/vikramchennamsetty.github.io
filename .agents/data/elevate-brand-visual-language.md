# ElevateLivingCo — Brand Visual Language & Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-29  

ElevateLivingCo's visual assets represent a high-end, editorial home decor brand. All generated images must maintain account-level coherence while providing visual diversity across individual Pinterest Pins.

---

## 1. Brand Visual Identity Attributes

- **Aesthetic Direction:** Premium, editorial, sophisticated, realistic, warm, architectural, lived-in.
- **Editorial Benchmark:** Architectural Digest, Elle Decor, House & Home luxury interior photography.
- **Color Philosophy:** Rich natural tones (espresso, warm ivory, taupe, oxblood, brass, sage, charcoal). Zero neon colors, zero digital oversaturation.
- **Surface & Material Finish:** Natural matte, satin wood finishes, brushed metals with subtle patina, uncompressed fabrics. Zero plastic CGI sheen.
- **Lighting Quality:** Physically plausible directional lighting (soft natural daylightfill + 2700K warm lamp pools). Zero flat flash or artificial HDR bloom.

---

## 2. Text-Safe Composition Rules

- **Zero In-Image AI Text:** Never attempt to generate text overlay inside the image generator prompt.
- **Reserved Negative Space:** Prompts MUST explicitly reserve one of 5 clean, low-contrast text-safe zones:
  1. `TOP_THIRD`: Clean upper wall wainscoting / plaster.
  2. `BOTTOM_THIRD`: Clean rug / floor / lower table surface.
  3. `LEFT_SIDE`: Vertical dark panel / wall wainscoting.
  4. `RIGHT_SIDE`: Vertical wall / drapes.
  5. `CENTER_LIGHT`: Soft ambient wall space.

---

## 3. Product-to-Editorial Ratio

- **Default Balance:** **80% Editorial Interior Inspiration / 20% Product Focus.**
- **Shopping Intent Exception:** For Pins targeting direct commercial buying intent (*"Shop Amazon Finds"*), product emphasis may increase up to 50% while strictly preserving natural, realistic interior room staging.
