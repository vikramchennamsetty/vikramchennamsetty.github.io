## 1. Timing Evidence Baseline & Studies

### A. Official Pinterest Guidance (Level A Evidence)
- Pinterest algorithms distribute fresh content asynchronously based on user search query matching, board activity, and visual indexation.
- Immediate real-time engagement provides initial signals, but high-performing Pins generate peak impressions over weeks and months (long-tail decay curve).

### B. Third-Party Niche Studies — Home Decor (Level C Evidence)
- **Primary US Active Windows (Home Decor & Lifestyle):**
  - **Evening Peak:** 08:00 PM – 11:00 PM EST (Peak leisure browsing window for US users after work/dinner).
  - **Late Afternoon:** 02:00 PM – 04:00 PM EST (Workplace break / afternoon discovery window).
  - **Weekend Morning:** 09:00 AM – 11:00 AM EST (Saturday/Sunday home planning window).
- **High-Performing Days for Home Decor:** Sunday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday.

## 3. Multi-Window Test Matrix for Campaigns

When scheduling a batch of 5 Pins for a campaign:
- **Pin 1:** Sunday — Prime Evening (08:00 PM EST / 05:30 AM IST)
- **Pin 2:** Tuesday — Afternoon (02:00 PM EST / 11:30 PM IST)
- **Pin 3:** Thursday — Morning (08:00 AM EST / 05:30 PM IST)
- **Pin 4:** Friday — Prime Evening (09:00 PM EST / 06:30 AM IST)
- **Pin 5:** Saturday — Weekend Morning (10:00 AM EST / 07:30 PM IST)

*Constraint: Ensure $\ge 72$ hours spacing between Pins sharing the exact same destination URL.*
